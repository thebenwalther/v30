# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, minimal JS by default). GSAP with ScrollTrigger and SplitText for scroll-driven and typographic motion. Lenis for smooth scrolling. One hand-written WebGL canvas (Three.js or OGL with a custom shader). Deploy target undecided: the build must stay a plain static site that runs on any host.

## Users

Adults who have a good life by any outside measure but are not fully present in it. There is something more they want, they keep thinking about it, and they never act on it. That unmet want pulls their attention out of the day they are actually living. They arrive curious, not in crisis.

## Product Purpose

The personal brand site of Ben Walther. It exists to make a visitor recognize themselves, trust that Ben has lived this, and take one next step: joining a weekly letter. Success is a visitor who reads to the end and subscribes.

## Positioning

The long-term business is practical tools and systems for acting on what you want, not coaching. The claim is that follow-through and presence are the same problem: acting on the want is what frees attention for today. The framing is "a good life you're not fully in," not "the wrong life."

## Operating Context

A one-page scroll story on phones and desktops, read in a quiet moment. The only conversion is an email signup for a weekly letter.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page only for now.
- Weekly letter signup: email provider not decided. The form must work accessibly and post to a clearly marked, swappable placeholder endpoint.
- No products, prices, courses, or tools exist on the site yet; none may be implied as available.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ben Walther.
- Hero headline (verbatim): "Follow through on what matters. Be present for the life you have."
- Hero subhead (verbatim): "Practical tools and systems to help you act on what you want, so it stops pulling your attention away from today."
- Next-step line (verbatim): "Turn the thing you keep thinking about into a next step."
- Voice: short, honest, plain. No hype.
- Pinned by the brief: no centered-headline-plus-two-buttons hero, no three-card feature rows, no gradient blobs, no stock photos, no generic testimonial strip. Not Inter for everything.

## Evidence on Hand

- Ben's story facts: 15 years as a teacher, coach, and IT director; meaningful work without being present for it; the realization built over time and clicked during self-reflection.
- A portrait of Ben will be supplied later. Until then, one intentional slot, gracefully empty.
- Absent and never to be fabricated: testimonials, subscriber counts, press, client names, results or statistics.

## Product Principles

1. Recognition before persuasion: the visitor should see themselves before they are asked for anything.
2. Presence is the point: follow-through is the means, not the goal.
3. Lived, not taught: Ben's authority comes from his own story, stated plainly.
4. One next step: every page ends in a single small action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion with a calm, complete static version. The hero must be readable within three seconds with no motion dependency. Strong Lighthouse scores and smooth performance on mobile are required.
